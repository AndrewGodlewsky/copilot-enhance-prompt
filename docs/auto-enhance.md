# Chat auto-enhance was retired

Enhance Prompt no longer rewrites or appends to prompts typed directly into Copilot chat. Use the canvas's **Enhance** action, review the result and choose **Send to Copilot**. The separate, opt-in **Enhance and send** action remains available.

The former Rules and AI hook modes, their implementation source, timing limits and recovery guidance are preserved in the [chat auto-enhance retirement note](chat-auto-enhance-retirement.md).
